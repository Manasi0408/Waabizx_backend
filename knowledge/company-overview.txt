WAABIZX - COMPANY OVERVIEW

WaabizX is a WhatsApp communication and marketing platform for businesses.

WaabizX helps businesses manage WhatsApp customer communication from a centralized web platform.

Who WaabizX is for:
- Businesses that use WhatsApp to talk to customers
- Teams that run marketing, support, or sales on WhatsApp
- Organizations that need campaigns, templates, contacts, and team inbox in one place

WaabizX is designed to simplify WhatsApp operations without requiring visitors to understand technical backend details.
